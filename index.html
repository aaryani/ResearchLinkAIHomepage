Making Australia's research capability visible.

ResearchLink.ai turns scattered research information into a connected map of who knows what, who has delivered what, and who works with whom.

A Swinburne University of Technology spin-out

The problem

Australia's research strengths are hard to see.

The knowledge of who can do what is spread across reports, PDFs and systems that don't talk to each other.

So finding the right expert, team or partner is slow, and often comes down to who you already know. Good collaborations are missed, and decisions about where to invest in research and development are made with less evidence than they deserve.

What we do

A connected map of national research capability.

ResearchLink.ai uses AI to bring fragmented research information together into one living picture of people, organisations, projects and the collaborations between them.

  1. Connect

    Research information from many sources is linked into a single network, so relationships that were buried become visible.

  2. Understand

    AI fills the gaps, works out who is who, and keeps the context, so the picture can be trusted.

  3. Ask

    Decision-makers ask questions in plain language, by voice or text, and get evidence-based answers with clear visual maps.
